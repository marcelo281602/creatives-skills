# Eria — look constants and drift tolerances

Use this when reviewing a generation. "Hard" means reject and regenerate; "soft" means acceptable if the rest of the
image is strong; "free" means it may vary between posts.

## Head and face

| Feature | Constant | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Age read | a woman around 31 | **Hard.** Anything that reads under 25 is rejected. |
| Skin | warm medium-brown, natural texture, visible pores | **Hard** on tone shifts of more than a step; soft on texture. |
| Eyes | hazel-amber | Soft. Brown is acceptable in low light; green or blue is not. |
| Brows | soft arched, natural density | Soft. |
| Beauty mark | one, small, just under the left eye | **Hard** when the left side of the face is visible. Mirrored selfies put it on the right — that is correct for a mirror. |
| Lips | matte terracotta | Soft. Glossy or bright red is rejected. |
| Expression | curious half-smile; open laugh allowed in talking clips | Free within warmth. Blank or sultry looks are rejected. |
| Smile lines | faint | Free. |

## Hair

| Feature | Constant | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | copper-red | **Hard.** Auburn-brown in shadow is fine; blonde, black or orange-cartoon is not. |
| Texture | loose curls | **Hard.** Straight or tight coils are rejected. |
| Length | shoulder | Soft (collarbone to just below shoulder). |
| Style | half-up in a tortoiseshell claw clip, loose curls at the temples | Soft. Fully down is allowed for the Coast and Evening looks. |

## Accessories

| Feature | Constant | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Glasses | round, thin brass wire rims, clear lenses | **Hard.** Thick, square, tinted or missing glasses are rejected. |
| Camera | small vintage half-frame film camera, woven olive strap | **Hard** when in frame. It may be out of frame in tight close-ups. Never a phone, never a DSLR. |
| Jewellery | small brass or gold-toned hoops | Free; nothing large or branded. |
| Tattoos | none | **Hard.** |

## Body and framing

- Neutral, non-sexual description only. No body-shape adjectives in prompts.
- Portraits at 35–50 mm equivalent; full-body at 28–35 mm. Wider lenses distort the face and push the age down.
- Hands: check finger count and how the camera is held. Regenerate warped hands rather than cropping them out if they
  carry the camera.

## Wardrobe and setting checks

- One named look per image (see the bible, step 3). Mixed looks are rejected unless the post is a "which look" carousel.
- No readable logos on clothing, cameras or props.
- Settings come from the recurring list. A new setting should share the warm, filmic grade.

## Grade

- Warm highlights, slightly lifted shadows, fine film grain.
- Night: warm practical lights over teal shadows, never a strong orange-and-teal split.
- Darkroom: red safelight is the only strong colour; skin should still read as skin, not pure red.
